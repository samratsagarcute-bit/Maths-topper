Squares and Square Roots 📐

Core Concept: Finding numbers multiplied by themselves and reversing the process to find original dimensions.

🌸 Real-Life Quest: You want to make a cozy square Korean-style study mat that covers an area of 144 square meters. How long should each side of your mat be?

Core Concept: Powers and laws of indices to simplify massive or tiny numbers neatly.
