📚 Important Chapters

1. Rational Numbers

Properties of rational numbers, representation on number line, and between two rational numbers.

2. Linear Equations in One Variable

Solving equations having linear expressions on one side and numbers on the other.

3. Algebraic Expressions and Identities

Terms, factors, coefficients, addition, subtraction, multiplication of algebraic expressions, and standard identities.

4. Mensuration

Area of trapezium, general quadrilaterals, surface area, and volume of cubes, cuboids, and cylinders.

🪄 Cute Formula Cheat-Sheet

Algebraic Identities

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b)

Mensuration Formulas

Area of Trapezium = ½ × (Sum of parallel sides) × height
Total Surface Area of Cylinder = 2πr(r + h)
Volume of Cylinder = πr²h
